Field Notes

Updates

A running log of meetings, deployments, findings, and milestones as IFEI develops.

July 2026 Milestone

First sensor calibrated

Our first conductivity sensor completed calibration this month, tested against a reference conductivity meter across a range of known standards. The calibration curve now converts raw sensor output directly into real specific conductance (µS/cm) — the USGS standard unit — bringing us from a working prototype to an instrument producing scientifically meaningful data. Full calibration methodology will be published on our GitHub alongside the firmware.

June 2026 Milestone

Engineering begins

With components sourced and partnerships in place, we moved from planning into hands-on hardware development. Circuit assembly, sensor housing design, and firmware development kicked off this month, building on an open-source conductivity measurement approach and adapting it for continuous, low-power field deployment in Northern Illinois lakes.

Week of May 18, 2026 Milestone

Initial partnership meetings

Met with Jeff Weiss of the Buffalo Grove Environmental Action Team and Karen Lange of the Flint & Spring Creek Watersheds Partnership to discuss our approach and get direct input from people already working to protect these watersheds. Both conversations shaped our thinking on which metrics matter most and confirmed there's a real, unmet need for continuous monitoring infrastructure at the local level.

May 11, 2026 Milestone

IFEI launches

The Illinois Fishery Ecosystem Initiative officially begins. Founded by two Buffalo Grove students who've spent years fishing Northern Illinois lakes, IFEI's mission is to build low-cost, open-source water quality sensors and put continuous data in the hands of the people managing these ecosystems.

Summer 2026 Planned

First lake deployment

Documentation of our first in-lake deployment — installation process, early readings, and lessons learned from real-world conditions. To be updated as this milestone is reached.

Fall 2026 Planned

First data analysis and findings

Summary of our first dataset — seasonal patterns, notable events, and what the data tells us about the health of monitored lakes. To be updated once data collection is underway.
